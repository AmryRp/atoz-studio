# AtoZ — overhead jade pond

The hero and contact backgrounds now use a straight-down view into a dark jade pond. Only submerged stones, pebbles, and weathered twigs are visible; there is no horizon or above-water scenery. The red Hannya mask floats above the water and submerges with caustics and ripples on hover or touch-and-hold.

The site shares an ink-green, jade, pale-mint, and restrained warm-gold palette across navigation, portfolio cards, viewer, and studio panel. Liquid reflections use pale jade and warm sunlight tones.

## Assets

- `public/portfolio/overhead-pond-desktop.jpg` — full-resolution desktop background.
- `public/portfolio/overhead-pond-mobile.jpg` — smaller responsive copy, framed toward the submerged stones.
- Original image generated with the built-in image_gen tool. JPEG optimization and proportional resizing use System.Drawing; no external image dependency was added.
- Previous dreamscape assets are retained but no longer referenced by the theme.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: high-resolution website background, landscape 16:9 canvas, but strictly TOP-DOWN AERIAL camera looking vertically down into water.
Primary request: An atmospheric clear dark jade pond surface viewed from directly above at 90 degrees. The whole frame is water and the underwater pond bed. Absolutely NO horizon, NO skyline, NO above-water landscape, NO shore or bank.
Underwater details: A sparse arrangement of rounded river stones, dark slate pebbles, and two or three slender weathered sunken twigs along the far edges and corners, gently obscured by depth. Very faint mineral texture in the pond bed. Natural shallow ripples with delicate broken warm sunlight caustics and cool emerald reflections. The middle and lower-left should be mostly quiet, dark, open water so a floating red Hannya mask and ivory website text can be composited over them. No large central stone or stick. Enough depth to feel like a real pond, but only submerged details are visible.
Composition: True flat-lay overhead bird's-eye view, orthographic-feeling, entire rectangular frame filled edge to edge with the same horizontal plane of water, no perspective horizon. Broad calm negative space in center-right for the mask and lower-left for text; safe central crop for mobile.
Style: Sophisticated cinematic photorealistic water environment for a 3D artist portfolio. Deep ink green and emerald-jade water, muted natural stone grey, weathered brown wood, restrained pale gold caustics. Dark and rich, readable behind light text. Natural realistic water, no artificial neon glow, no purple tones.
Constraints: No sky, no mountains, no forest silhouettes, no horizon, no land above water, no shoreline, no banks, no plants above the surface, no mask, no faces, no people, no animals, no blood, no text, no logos, no UI, no borders, no watermark. Background art only.
